# Glossary

Selected terms for navigation. Roman numerals are book chapters; A-C are appendices. For assumptions and full statements, read the linked chapter from the master index.

- **Affine scheme** - A locally ringed space isomorphic to `Spec A`. (II)
- **Ample** - On a noetherian scheme, an invertible sheaf L such that F tensor L^n is globally generated for every coherent F and all sufficiently large n. (II)
- **Arithmetic genus** - `(-1)^n(chi(O_X)-1)` for a projective scheme of dimension `n`. (I, III)
- **Base change** - Replacement of a base through a fibre product; cohomology comparison requires additional hypotheses. (II, III)
- **Base point** - A point where all sections of a selected linear system vanish. (II, IV)
- **Birational** - Isomorphic on dense opens, equivalently having isomorphic function fields for varieties. (I)
- **Blowup** - The relative Proj of an ideal sheaf's Rees algebra. (II, V)
- **Canonical sheaf** - `det Omega_{X/k}` for a nonsingular variety. (II)
- **Cartier divisor** - Local meromorphic equations with invertible ratios on overlaps. (II)
- **Cech cohomology** - Cohomology of the alternating intersection-restriction complex of a cover. (III)
- **Chern class** - A characteristic cycle class associated to a vector bundle. (A)
- **Chow group** - Algebraic cycles modulo rational equivalence, graded by codimension here. (A)
- **Coherent sheaf** - On noetherian schemes, a sheaf locally associated to a finitely generated module. (II)
- **Complete variety** - A variety proper over its ground field. (II)
- **Curve** - In IV, a nonsingular projective one-dimensional variety over an algebraically closed field. (IV)
- **Degree** - A divisor's weighted point count on a curve, or the normalized leading Hilbert coefficient for an embedding. (I, II)
- **Dualizing sheaf** - A sheaf with trace representing top-degree cohomological duality. (III)
- **Effective divisor** - A Weil divisor with nonnegative coefficients; a Cartier divisor locally defined by regular non-zero-divisors. (II)
- **Elliptic curve** - In Hartshorne, a genus-one curve; an origin supplies its group law. (IV)
- **Euler characteristic** - The alternating sum of finite cohomology dimensions. (III)
- **Exceptional curve** - The projective line replacing a blown-up smooth surface point. (V)
- **Flat** - Tensoring with the module preserves exactness; applied stalkwise for sheaves and morphisms. (III)
- **Formal completion** - The formal scheme assembled from successive infinitesimal neighborhoods of a closed subscheme. (II)
- **Frobenius** - The characteristic-`p` power endomorphism; specify the power and base-field convention. (IV, C)
- **Function field** - The field of rational functions on an integral variety or scheme. (I, II)
- **GAGA** - Algebraic-analytic comparison for coherent sheaves and cohomology in its proper/projective complex setting. (B)
- **Genus** - `h^1(O_X)=h^0(omega_X)` for a nonsingular projective curve. (IV)
- **Geometric fibre** - A fibre extended to an algebraic closure of its residue field. (III)
- **Hilbert polynomial** - The eventual polynomial describing graded dimensions or twisted Euler characteristics. (I, III)
- **Hyperelliptic** - A genus-at-least-two curve admitting a degree-two morphism to `P^1`. (IV)
- **Integral scheme** - A nonempty irreducible reduced scheme. (II)
- **Invertible sheaf** - A locally free sheaf of rank one, also called a line bundle. (II)
- **Irreducible** - Nonempty and not a union of two proper closed subsets. (I)
- **Jacobian** - The abelian variety parametrizing degree-zero line bundles on a curve. (IV, B)
- **Kodaira dimension** - Pluricanonical growth invariant; Hartshorne uses `-1` for the vanishing case. (V)
- **Linear equivalence** - Divisors differ by a principal divisor. (II)
- **Linear system** - A projective system of effective divisors obtained from sections of a line bundle. (II, IV)
- **Locally factorial** - All local rings are unique factorization domains. (II)
- **Moishezon** - Compact complex manifold with meromorphic function field of maximal transcendence degree. (B)
- **Normal** - Every local ring is an integrally closed domain. (II)
- **Numerical equivalence** - Divisors have equal intersection numbers with all curves. (V)
- **Picard group** - Isomorphism classes of invertible sheaves under tensor product. (II)
- **Plurigenus** - `P_m=h^0(O_X(mK_X))`. (V)
- **Proper** - Separated, finite type, and universally closed. (II)
- **Quasi-coherent** - Locally the sheaf associated to a module on an affine chart. (II)
- **Ramification divisor** - For separable curve maps, the divisor of lengths of relative differentials. (IV)
- **Rational equivalence** - Cycle relation generated by principal divisors on subvarieties. (A)
- **Reduced** - Local rings have no nonzero nilpotents. (II)
- **Regular local ring** - Noetherian local ring with embedding dimension equal to Krull dimension. (I)
- **Separated** - The relative diagonal is a closed immersion. (II)
- **Special divisor** - A divisor `D` with `h^1(O_X(D))>0`. (IV)
- **Strict transform** - Closure of the inverse image away from a blowup's center. (V)
- **Surface** - In V, a nonsingular projective two-dimensional variety over an algebraically closed field. (V)
- **Very ample** - Realizes a closed immersion into projective space, relative to the specified base. (II)
- **Weil divisor** - An integral codimension-one cycle, with divisor theory under II.6's hypotheses. (II)
- **Zeta function** - `exp(sum_{r>=1} #X(F_{q^r}) t^r/r)`. (C)
